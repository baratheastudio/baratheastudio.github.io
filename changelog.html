1.0.0 First release, November 2026

The first version of Barathea, with two presets: Barathea and Plaster.

In this version

  • Story and shop: Shop the look in three layouts (spread, full screen, compact), with rings that mark each product in the picture on pointer and keyboard focus, optional dots, and a true "in this picture" count. Also as a grid tile and inside journal stories.
  • The index: header menu panels with lines and one preview picture; Collection index; the line intro's index of product types; Index A-Z on the Collections page.
  • Client care with context: one panel from the header, phone menu, product page, sold-out sizes, quick add, bag, footer, search and size guide, carrying the product, color, size and style code into messages and the contact form.
  • Size before buy: no preselected size, Add to bag and express checkout wait for a size, sold-out sizes lead to Ask about this size, a phone buy bar that follows the same rule.
  • Promises per market: delivery times, returns windows and duties for up to three country groups and every other country, the same in the bag, on the product page and on the Shipping and returns page; the promises line; announcement messages by country.
  • Final sale: by tag, collection or personalization, with a line above Add to bag, on bag lines and as a line item property.
  • Arrange a visit: through your contact form with a visit topic, or your own booking link.
  • The card: one product card everywhere, with the line, a material line, real badges, native swatches, quick add by size and quick view for long size runs.
  • Product pages: four templates (default, bag, jewelry, jewelry without personalization), pictures per color, full-screen zoom, size guide drawer, fit line, personalization, specifications from a metafield, pickup availability, Complete the look and You may also like.
  • Lists: category and line templates, tiles in the grid, Search and Discovery filters with quick filters, three loading styles, predictive search with style code matching.
  • Pages: templates for About, Client care, Contact, Boutiques, FAQ, Size guide, Shipping and returns, Gifting, Personalization, Services and Workroom; a journal with shoppable story templates.
  • Bag: a drawer, a bag page or a short confirmation; gift wrapping, a gift message and leaving prices out of the parcel; delivery notes.
  • English storefront and editor text; a layout that mirrors for right-to-left languages.
