Setup

Does the theme need any apps?

No. Everything in these docs works without an app. Filters and the Complete the look products come from Shopify's free Search and Discovery app, which Shopify makes.

The Client care link does not show.

It hides until at least one Client care setting is filled in. Add a phone number, a message link, an email address or a contact page in Theme settings > Client care, and check that Show the Client care link is on in the header.

How do I put the header over the first picture?

In the header section, set Over the first section on the home page to Phones only or All sizes. Use it when the home page opens with a full-width picture. A line intro in a dark scheme placed first on a line page gets the same header, and so does an Image banner with its text beside the picture, in a dark scheme only.

How do I add a second button to the home opener?

Select the home page's first section (in the preset, the Image banner; Shop the look works the same way), fill Second link label and Second link, and set Link style to Buttons: the first link is filled, the second an outline. A link shows only when it has both a label and a link. Text links shows the same two links as underlined text. See Links and buttons.

The text over my home picture is hard to read.

On a pale or busy picture, take the text off it. In the Image banner, set Text position to Beside the image: on computers the text stands in its own column beside the picture, and the picture and both buttons show on the first screen; on phones and tablets the text sits beneath the picture. Add a portrait crop of the picture as its Phone image. See Text beside the picture. To keep the text over a picture, raise Shade behind text over images in Theme settings > Colors.

The header shows my store name instead of a logo.

Add your logo in Theme settings > Logo, and a light version as Logo on dark grounds for the header over pictures and on ink sections.

The country or language selector does not show.

It appears in the footer and the phone menu when your store sells in more than one country (Settings > Markets) or language (Settings > Languages), and the selector is on in the Footer and Header sections.

The account icon does not show.

Turn on customer accounts in Settings > Customer accounts.

Follow on Shop does not show.

It needs Shop Pay turned on in your payment settings, and Show Follow on Shop on in the Footer or Email signup section.

Can shoppers book a time?

Not in the theme: Shopify does not allow themes to take bookings. Plan a visit sends shoppers to your contact form with the visit topic, or to your own page about visiting if you add a Visit page link in Theme settings > Client care. See Plan a visit.

The announcement bar says "Welcome to our store".

That is its starting message. In the theme editor, select the Announcement bar section in the header and write your own message, or remove the section. Each message can show in some countries only, so a delivery line appears only where it is true.

Is there a wish list?

Not built in; wish lists are app work. See Apps that pair well.

Products and sizes

Why does Add to bag say "Select a size"?

Nothing is added until the shopper chooses a size. The button moves to the sizes when pressed. A product with only one size in stock has it chosen for the shopper (Theme settings > Product page > Select a size for the shopper). Only options named in Size option names (same place) count as a size, and gift cards never ask for one. If a product without sizes asks for a size, its option name is on that list: remove it there.

Why are the express checkout buttons dimmed?

They wait for a size, with the line "Select a size to use express checkout.", so nobody buys a size they did not pick. To let them work at once, turn off Wait for a size before express checkout in Theme settings > Product page. Add to bag still waits.

Express checkout buttons do not show at all.

They depend on the payment methods turned on in Settings > Payments, and on Show accelerated checkout buttons in the Buy buttons block.

A sold-out size can still be bought.

The variant's stock is not tracked, or Continue selling when out of stock is on. Turn on Track quantity and turn the other off.

Does the theme support B2B quantity rules and volume pricing?

Yes. For a signed-in B2B customer, the quantity field follows the variant's minimum, maximum and increment, the rule shows under it ("Minimum of 2 / Increments of 2 / Maximum of 10"), price breaks show as a Volume pricing table, and the bag follows the same rule. A product with a minimum above 1 opens quick view from quick add. See Quantity rules and volume pricing.

Can I hide the quantity field?

Yes. The quantity field shows by default, in every product template. To hide it on a template, open that product template in the theme editor, select the Buy buttons block and turn off Show quantity. Shoppers can still change the quantity in the bag, and the minimum of a B2B quantity rule is still added.

How do I show "Size (FR)"?

Type FR into Size system label in Theme settings > Product page. A template can use its own label, for example mm for rings, in its Colors and sizes block.

The Size guide link does not show.

Pick a size guide page in the Size guide block, in Theme settings > Product page or in Client care. See Size guide.

Cards show "3 colors" instead of swatches.

Your color option has no swatch data. Link it to a color metafield or give each value a swatch in the product; see Swatches. Or Colors is set to Number of colors in Theme settings > Product cards, as the preset sets it: choose Swatches there.

The gallery shows every color's pictures.

Turn on Show only the chosen color's pictures in Product information, set a variant picture for each color, and order the media by color. See Pictures per color.

How do I add the line under the product name?

Fill the product's Subtitle metafield (descriptors.subtitle), after turning on the standard definition in Settings > Custom data. See Metafields.

Earrings show the Personalization field.

Give earrings and chains the product.jewelry-plain template; it is the jewelry page without Personalization and without the Size guide link.

Collections and lines

Filters do not show on my collection pages.

Add filters in the Search and Discovery app (Filters, then Add filter), and check that Show filters is on in the Product grid.

The line intro's index lists nothing, or its links show every product.

The index lists the product types in the line and links through the Product type filter. Fill in product types and add the Product type filter in Search and Discovery.

A product shows the wrong line, or none.

A product's line is the first collection in your Lines menu that holds it, in menu order. Check that the menu is picked in Theme settings > Lines and that the product is in the line's collection. For products in no line, choose what shows in When a product is in no line.

Shop the look shows no rings on a line page.

On a template shared by several lines, a Shop the look set to This collection prints no rings and counts "From this line". Give the line its own template and set each product's point there. On phones, rings are hidden when a phone picture is set.

How do I show the number of products in the Collection index?

In the section, under Rows, turn on Show the number of products. Each row reads, for example, "7 styles"; a collection with no products shows no count. To use another word, change it in Edit default theme content. See Rows of the Collection index.

A grid tile does not show.

Tiles print nothing until they have a picture (or text, or a film), show on the first load only by default, and hide while filters are applied. Check its Place after product number against the number of products.

Sale badges do not show.

Sale badge may be set to None in Theme settings > Product cards; choose Word (as the preset does) or Percentage off. A Sale badge also needs a compare-at price higher than the price, allowed in the shopper's market.

Some shoppers do not see the compare-at price.

Check Theme settings > Prices: compare-at prices can be limited to some countries, to your store currency, or hidden for products with a tag.

Bag and promises

The promises line does not show.

The theme ships no promises of its own. Type up to three in Theme settings > Bag, only what your store keeps (for example your delivery time or your returns window), and check that Show the promises is on. An empty field shows nothing.

The gift options have no gift wrapping.

Gift wrapping shows only when you offer it: turn on Offer gift wrapping in Theme settings > Bag. "This is a gift", the gift message and leaving prices out of the parcel show without it.

The delivery line does not show in the bag.

Fill in a delivery time, a returns window or duties for the shopper's country, or for Every other country, in Theme settings > Shipping and returns. A part left empty is not shown.

Why does a product say "Final sale"?

It has your final sale tag (default final-sale), is in a final sale collection, or is personalized while Personalized items are final sale is on. See Final sale.

How do I send shoppers to the bag page instead of the drawer?

In Theme settings > Bag, set After adding to the bag to Go to the bag page, or to Show a short confirmation.

Where do shoppers enter a discount code?

At checkout. Discounts already applied show in the bag.

License and updates

Can I use the theme on more than one store?

A purchase is for one store. Each store needs its own.

How do I update without losing my work?

Your settings and content move to the new version; code edits do not. See Updating safely.

Can I edit the code?

Yes, in Online Store > Themes > ... > Edit code, on a duplicate first. Code edits are not covered by support and are not carried into updates.
