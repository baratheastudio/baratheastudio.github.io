The preset

Barathea ships with one look, a preset also named Barathea, made for a shop that sells bags and shoes from many designers. This page lists what the preset sets and how to get the look of its demo store.

On this page

A preset sets the look: colors, fonts, the product card, layout values, the header and footer groups and a set of templates. It never sets store content. Menus, pages, collections, Client care numbers, delivery times and your words stay yours, so the demo store was filled by hand; its checklist is summed up below.

The look

A multi-designer shop of bags and shoes. Demo store: Ostrel, with six designers (Belsade, Ilvero, Odrane, Quarne, Tessaly, Wenlow) and 42 products: bags, small leather goods and shoes.

  • Grounds: concrete page ground, white quiet ground, ink and stone. No accent color: the service voice is ink italic with its glyph.
  • Type: Libre Caslon Text for names, headings and body; Archivo for labels, menu, buttons and prices, in capitals. Small headings.
  • Header: solid white and always in view, never over a picture. The announcement bar is ink and shows on the home page too.
  • Cards: square packshots shown whole on a white ground, the designer (vendor) above the name, the second picture on hover, a count of colors instead of swatches, and a Sale badge.
  • Home: Image banner with its text beside the picture on computers and beneath it (16:9) on phones and tablets, with a short text and two buttons, Shop new in and Our designers; Featured products (New in); Collection index named Designers, each row with what the designer makes, its number of products, an arrow and hairlines, square small pictures on phones and All designers under the list; Two images; Featured products (Shoes); Feature with text on the stone ground.
  • Designers: the Collections page is Index A-Z with letter jumps and a preview picture. Designer pages use the line template with a light line intro, a four-column grid without tiles, and More designers.
  • Product pages: a square gallery that shows the whole picture, the designer above the name, and the quantity field beside Add to bag.
  • Client care panel: short: call, email, size guide. Add the other items when your store offers them (see Client care with context).

The preset also keeps low stock off, asks for a size before express checkout, shows the buy bar on phones, opens the bag drawer after an add, shows gift options without gift wrapping (Offer gift wrapping off), ships the three promises empty (the promises line shows nothing until you type yours), shows the quantity field in every product template, and uses square corners.

Color schemes

The preset sets four schemes, each with a role: scheme 1 the page ground, scheme 2 a quiet ground, scheme 3 the night ground (dark; pictures and their grounds stay light), scheme 4 the season ground. Text over pictures uses scheme 3.

Every scheme also sets quiet text, hairlines, control lines and an image ground (white in all four). Each pair of text and ground was checked for contrast: at least 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for field lines. If you change colors, keep to the same ratios; the editor does not check them for you.

Getting the demo look

The preset gives you the frame; the pictures and content make the look.

  • Square packshots on a pure white background (#FFFFFF), at least 1600 px on each side. The cards show the whole picture on a white ground, so any other background shows as a box.
  • Set each product's vendor to its designer, and make one collection per designer (a smart collection by vendor works). Give every designer collection a picture: the Designers index on phones shows small pictures only when every designer has one.
  • Make a Designers menu of those collections; pick it as the Lines menu and as the menu of the Collections page (Index A-Z, Collections from a menu).
  • In the home Designers section, pick a designer's collection in each Collection block (the preset brings six, with the demo store's lines) and rewrite each Line under the name: what that designer makes, in a few words. See Rows of the Collection index.
  • Point the opener's buttons at your own pages: the demo store sends Shop new in to its New in collection and Our designers to its Our designers page. The preset sends them to all products and the Collections page, which every store has. All designers, under the Designers list, goes to the Collections page: in this look, the Designers index A-Z.
  • Give the home Image banner a wide picture (16:9) and, as its Phone image, a portrait crop of the same picture (4:5). Computers show the portrait one beside the text, phones show it in place of the wide one, and tablets show the wide one. See Text beside the picture.
  • Add the Vendor filter in Search and Discovery and label it "Designer".
  • Type your own promises in Theme settings > Bag (the demo store types its delivery time and returns window there) and turn on Offer gift wrapping if you wrap orders.

Making it your own

Every value on this page is a setting: fonts and heading size in Theme settings > Typography, the four grounds in Theme settings > Colors, the card in Theme settings > Product cards. The theme's other sections stay available in the editor whatever the preset uses: for example Shop the look for campaign pictures with their products, or portrait cards and a Lines menu for a house with lines of its own (see Collections and lines).
