Signature features

Nine systems give Barathea its character. Each is built from ordinary sections and settings, so you can use as much or as little of it as your store needs.

Story and shop

A campaign picture and the products in it, shown together. When a shopper points at a product, or reaches it with the keyboard, a fine ring appears where that product sits in the picture. The credit counts what is shown: "3 in this picture".

Where it appears

  • The Shop the look section, in three layouts: Spread (the picture at full height beside its products), Full screen (the picture fills the screen, products beneath) and Compact (for line pages and stories). First on the home page, its heading is the page heading.
  • The Shop the look tile block of the product grid, placed among the cards.
  • The Shop the look block inside a journal story.

Set it up

  1. Add the section and pick a portrait picture with the products in it. Set its focal point in Content > Files so the crop keeps the products.
  2. Add one Product block for each product the picture shows, no more and no fewer: the count comes from the blocks.
  3. In each Product block, set From the left and From the top (in percent) to the product's place in the picture. The ring appears there.
  4. Optional: Show dots on the picture adds visible dots that lead to their product when tapped. Off keeps the picture clear.

Good to know

A phone picture is a different picture, so the rings and dots are hidden on phones when you set one. With Products: This collection on a line template shared by several lines, the section uses each collection's own picture and products; it then prints no rings and the count reads "From this line". For rings on a line page, give that line its own template.

Links and buttons

The credit of a Shop the look or an Image banner section takes up to two links. Each one shows only when it has both a label and a link. Link style sets how they look:

  • Text links (the default): underlined links after the text and the count, the quiet look of a credit under a picture.
  • Buttons: a row under the text, the first link as a filled button and the second as an outline button. It suits the section that opens the home page. The second button is always an outline, whatever Theme settings > Buttons > Secondary actions is set to, so the two read as a pair whatever that setting is.

The buttons are as tall as every other button (Button and field height in Theme settings > Buttons, 44 px at least). On phones they share one row, each half the width, and a long label wraps inside its button; in the Spread layout of Shop the look they stay on the first screen. Over a picture they sit on the same soft shade as the text, and the outline button keeps a little of that shade behind its label so it reads on a light picture too.

Add a second button

  1. In the theme editor, open the home page and select its first section: in the preset, the Image banner. A Shop the look you put first works the same way.
  2. Fill Link label and Link with the main way in, for example "Shop new in" and your New in collection.
  3. Fill Second link label and Second link, for example "Our designers" and your designers page.
  4. Set Link style to Buttons. Keep both labels short, two or three words, so each fits its half of the row on a phone.

Switch to text links

Set Link style to Text links. Both links stay, as underlined text after the credit; to keep only one, empty the second label or link. Nothing else changes.

Text beside the picture

An Image banner sets its credit (the top line, heading, text and links) beneath the picture, over it, or beside it. Set Text position to Beside the image and, on a laptop or a larger screen, the whole picture and the whole credit show together on the first screen, side by side, and neither covers the other.

On computers
From laptop width (990 px), the credit stands in a column of its own, centered on the height of the picture, and the picture takes the other side. The picture is as tall as the first screen allows under the announcement bar and the header, so the heading, the text and both buttons show without scrolling, on a 1280 x 800 laptop as on a 1920 x 1080 screen. The picture stays inside the page margins.
On phones and tablets
Below laptop width it is the same as Beneath the image: the picture first, in the section's Shape and Full width, then the credit under it. With the text beside the picture, those two settings apply to phones and tablets only.
Text side
Start (the default) puts the credit on the left and the picture on the right; End swaps them. In a right-to-left language, Start is the right side. Phones always show the picture first.
The picture it shows
Beside the text, the section shows a portrait picture. With a Phone image set, computers show that one too, in its own shape: a portrait crop of the same picture (4:5) is what it is made for. Without one, the main image is cropped to 4:5 around its focal point; set the focal point in Content > Files so the crop keeps the subject.

A row of products, when Show products beneath is on, still follows under the picture and the credit. Placed first on the home page, the picture is still the first one to load and the credit's heading is still the page heading. With a phone image, phones and computers each download one picture.

When to pick it

  • The text is hard to read over the picture: a pale ground, such as a white wall, linen or sand, or a busy one.
  • Text over the picture would cover what it shows, a bag or a pair of shoes, on a phone.
  • Beneath a wide picture, the buttons start below the first screen of a laptop.

Keep Beneath the image for a banner further down the page, and Over the image for a dark, quiet picture with room for the words. The preset's home opens with an Image banner beside its picture, Text side Start.

Put the text beside the picture

  1. In the theme editor, select the Image banner: in the preset, the home page's first section.
  2. Set Text position to Beside the image, and Text side to Start or End.
  3. Set a Phone image: a portrait crop (4:5) of the same picture, at least 1600 px on the long side. Computers show it beside the text and phones show it in place of the wide one.
  4. Preview the page at laptop width and on a phone. Keep the heading to two lines or fewer and the button labels short, two or three words.

Good to know

A header set to sit over the first section (Over the first section on the home page) does so over a banner with its text beside the picture only in a dark color scheme, such as the night scheme; on computers the picture and the credit then start below the header. In a light color scheme the header stays solid above the banner, on phones too, so its light text is never printed on a light ground.

The index

Names come first. Menus, lines and designers are set as a text index, and one picture beside them follows the name under the pointer or keyboard focus. The picture never holds a link and loads only when a shopper first shows interest, so the index stays light.

Where it appears

  • The header's menu panels. Each top-level menu item opens a panel with its links, the lines from your Lines menu (Show lines in menu panels) and one portrait picture. A Menu panel block in the header gives each item its first picture; otherwise the item's own collection picture is used.
  • The Collection index section, on the home page or anywhere. On phones each name can carry a small picture instead (shown only when every name has one).
  • The Line intro at the top of a line page: the line's product types (or a menu) with counts.
  • The Collections page as Index A-Z: names in alphabetical groups, with letter jumps and a preview picture on wide screens.

Set it up

Give every collection a picture (portrait 4:5 works best) and a short description; the index uses both. Build your main menu in Online Store > Navigation, up to three levels. See Collections and lines.

Rows of the Collection index

Each name in a Collection index is one link across the whole row. The section's settings under Rows, Pictures and Link under the list say what else the rows carry:

Show one line under each name
A short line in a quieter type: the block's Line under the name, or else the start of the collection's description. A few words read best, for example "Structured bags and pumps".
Show the number of products
The collection's product count under the name, for example "7 styles" ("1 style" for one). A collection with no products shows no count. Off by default.
Show an arrow at the end of each row
A small arrow at the far end of the row, so the whole width reads as a link. It moves a little when the row is pointed at or reached by keyboard, and points the other way in right-to-left languages. Off by default.
Show lines between rows
A hairline above the first row and under every row, in the color scheme's hairline color. Off by default.
Small image shape
The shape of the small pictures on phones: Portrait 4:5 (72 px wide, the default) or Square (80 px). They show the block's Preview image, or else the collection's picture, cropped around its focal point, and only when every row has one.
Link label and Link
One link after the last row, for example "All designers" to the page that lists every designer. With the link empty, nothing shows; with the label empty, it reads "View all". It takes the secondary style of Theme settings > Buttons: a text link in the preset, an outline button if you choose Outline button there.

The line, the count, the arrow, the hairlines and the link show at every screen size. On computers the preview picture still follows the pointer beside the list; the small pictures are for phones.

Show product counts

  1. In the theme editor, select the Collection index section.
  2. Under Rows, turn on Show the number of products. Each row now counts its collection's products.
  3. To change the word, open Online Store > Themes > ... > Edit default theme content and search for "styles". The count has two forms, one for 1 ("1 style") and one for any other number ("7 styles"); change the word in both and leave the number's place as it is.

Client care with context

One panel holds every way to reach your team: call (with your hours), a message link, email, write to us, Plan a visit, boutiques and the size guide. The preset's panel starts short, with call, email and the size guide; add the others as blocks when your store offers them. It opens from the header, the phone menu, the product page (Ask an adviser), a sold-out size (Ask about this size), quick add, the bag, the footer, a search with no results and the size guide. When opened from a product, it starts with a line about what the shopper is looking at:

About: Pump 85, Black, EU 38, Style IL-3008-BLK-38

Set it up

  1. Fill in Theme settings > Client care: phone number, opening hours, message link, email address, Visit page link, and the Contact, Boutiques and Size guide pages. Every item hides while its setting is empty; with nothing filled in, the Client care link hides too.
  2. In the header group, the Client care panel section sets the order of the items with its blocks. Its setting Show what the shopper asks about turns the About line on or off.
  3. Make a Contact page with the contact template. Its Contact form has one Topic block per topic; the Topic code lets a link choose it (Client care uses visit, repair, advice, personalization and order).

What the context does

  • Message link. In the link, {context} becomes the About line and {ref} the place the chat started (header, product, size, bag, footer). For example https://wa.me/15550100?text={context}.
  • Write to us opens your contact page with the topic chosen and the About line filled in. The form sends it to you as a field named About, so you know which product, color and size the message is about.
  • The style code is the SKU of the chosen variant.

Pacing and the night band

Barathea separates sections with space, a change of ground or a change of picture, never with divider lines. A page reads as a rhythm: in the preset, products on the concrete page ground and on white, and the stone ground near the end of the page; an ink section changes the pace when a page needs one.

  • Color schemes carry the rhythm. Scheme 1 is the page ground, scheme 2 a quiet ground, scheme 3 the night ground and scheme 4 the season ground; every section picks one. Pictures stay light on the night ground.
  • The night band is the Image banner added from Night banner: a wide picture in the night scheme with its credit and a row of products beneath. The preset's home page has none; use one per page at most.
  • Scale sets pace too: Large text for one sentence, Product spotlight for one product alone on a plain field.
  • Space between sections (Theme settings > Layout) scales every section's Small, Medium and Large spacing at once.
  • Text over pictures uses the scheme chosen in Theme settings > Colors, on a soft shade whose strength you set there.

The card

One product card is used everywhere products are listed: grids, rows, Shop the look, search and the product page's rows. Set it once in Theme settings > Product cards.

  • Picture: square (the preset), portrait 4:5 or tall 3:4; edge to edge, or whole on the scheme's image ground, or blended so white photo backgrounds melt into that ground. A second picture on hover loads only when a shopper points at the card, on devices with a mouse.
  • Top line: the product's vendor (the preset), line or type, above the name.
  • Material line: from the product's Subtitle metafield (descriptors.subtitle), or the type or vendor.
  • Badges: Sold out and Sale are automatic; Sale shows only when a compare-at price is higher and allowed in the shopper's market. Add others with product tags that start with badge:, for example badge:New or badge:Made to order. Up to two show.
  • Colors: a count of colors (the preset), or Shopify's own color and image swatches. Choosing a color changes the card's picture and its quick add sizes.
  • Quick add: one button opens the sizes of the color shown, and nothing is added before a size is chosen. Products with more than 8 sizes or more than 2 options open a quick view sheet instead, and so does a product with a minimum quantity above 1 (B2B quantity rules), so the shopper sees the rule before adding. See Quantity rules and volume pricing.

Each card has one link, the product name, stretched over the whole card, so keyboard users meet one stop per product.

Size before buy

Nothing can be bought in a size nobody chose.

  • It applies to sizes only: options named in Theme settings > Product page > Size option names (Size, Taille, Ring size and others, in any case). Color, Material, a gift card's amount and any other option start on the first choice in stock, so a gift card or a product without sizes reads Add to bag at once.
  • No size is preselected, unless the link names a variant or only one size is in stock (Theme settings > Product page > Select a size for the shopper).
  • Until a size is chosen, Add to bag reads Select a size and adds nothing. Pressing it moves to the sizes.
  • Express checkout buttons wait, dimmed, with the line "Select a size to use express checkout." (Product page > Wait for a size before express checkout; the buttons keep their own colors.)
  • The buy bar on phones, quick add and quick view follow the same rule.
  • Sold-out sizes stay visible and can be chosen; choosing one shows Ask about this size, which opens Client care with the product, color, size and style code. Screen readers hear "EU 38, sold out, ask about this size".
  • The size label names its system, for example "Size (EU)", from Size system label. Low stock shows only if you turn it on, only for tracked inventory.
  • Without JavaScript, a plain list of sizes stands in, and the shopper still has to choose one.

Plan a visit

Shoppers can ask to visit your shop or a boutique. Shopify does not allow themes to take bookings, and Barathea takes none: Plan a visit is a plain link to a place you choose.

  • With a Visit page link in Theme settings > Client care (a page of your own about visiting you: where you are, when you are open, how to find you), Plan a visit goes there.
  • Without one, it opens your contact page with the topic visit chosen. The form has a free-text Best time to reach you field and, if you list them, a choice of boutique. There is no date or time picker.

Set it up

  1. In the Contact form section, keep a Topic block named "Plan a visit" with the topic code visit (it is there by default). Topics are yours to rename and reorder.
  2. For a page of its own, create a page "Plan a visit" with the contact template and put the visit topic first. Menus can link to /pages/plan-a-visit?topic=visit.
  3. The Plan a visit block of the Client care panel, the Boutiques section and the Address and hours section all use the same path, unless you give them a link of their own (Visit page link on a boutique).

Promises per market

Delivery times, returns windows and duties differ by country. Barathea keeps them in one place, so the bag, the product page and your Shipping and returns page always agree.

  1. In Theme settings > Shipping and returns, give up to three groups of countries (two-letter codes, for example AE, SA, KW) a delivery time, a returns window and a duties choice: Do not mention, We pay them, Included in the price, or May be due on delivery. Then fill in Every other country. A part left empty is not shown. Never type prices or currencies here.
  2. Shoppers then see one line for their own country, in the bag and under Add to bag: "Delivery to France in 2-4 working days / Returns within 14 days / Duties and import taxes on us".
  3. The Shipping and returns section prints the whole table on a page, with your shipping and refund policies.

Two more places follow the shopper's country:

  • The promises line. Up to three short promises in Theme settings > Bag, shown in the bag and under Add to bag, for example your delivery time or your returns window. The theme ships none and adds no wording of its own: type only what your store keeps. An empty field shows nothing, and Show the promises off removes the line. On the product page, the Promises and help block can show each promise in some countries only.
  • The announcement bar. Each message has Show in countries.

Compare-at prices can follow the market as well: see Theme settings > Prices.

Final sale

A product that cannot be returned says so, quietly, before it is bought: "Final sale. This item can't be returned." with a link to your refund policy. The line shows above Add to bag and on the product's line in the bag, and a line item property "Final sale: Yes" carries it to checkout and the order.

A product is final sale when, in Theme settings > Shipping and returns:

  • it has the Product tag (default final-sale, in any letter case), or
  • it is in one of the Final sale collections, or
  • Personalized items are final sale is on and the shopper personalizes it.

Final sale line changes the wording; left empty, the standard line follows the shopper's language.
