Set up your store

Journal

Your journal is a Shopify blog. Barathea gives it a quiet index page and a story template that places pictures, chapters and the products in them between the paragraphs.

The blog page

Create a blog in Online Store > Blog posts > Manage blogs, for example "Journal". Its page uses the Journal section: the newest story opens the first page, larger and with its standfirst, then the rest in a grid. Choose stories per page, the picture shape, and whether topics, date, author and excerpt show.

Writing a story

Title
The headline.
Excerpt
The standfirst under the headline, and the text on cards.
Image
The cover. Give it alt text; with Show the cover caption on, the alt text is printed as its caption.
Tags
Shown as topics, and usable by the Journal posts section to show one topic only.
Author
Shown when Show author is on.

Two article templates

article (default)
The Story section with the whole article text in one block. For news and short posts.
article.story
A shoppable story: the article text in parts, with a picture, a chapter heading, a Shop the look, a pull quote, two pictures and a row of products between them. After the story, a Shop the look section ("From this story") and Journal posts ("More from the journal").

Splitting the text into parts

In the article editor, add a horizontal line wherever a picture or products should go. In the story template, each Article text block shows one part: part 1 is the text before the first line, part 2 the text after it, and so on. Part 0 shows the whole article. Put your blocks between the parts in the theme editor.

The story's blocks are: Article text, Chapter heading, Text, Picture, Two pictures, Pull quote, Products (inline cards, or a scrolling row), Shop the look (a picture with the products in it and their count), Custom Liquid, and app blocks.

A template per story

A template carries one set of pictures and products, so each shoppable story needs its own: in the theme editor, choose Create template based on story, fill its pictures and products, then pick it for the article under Theme template. Stories that only need text and their cover can share the plain article template.

Keep the count true

In a Shop the look block or section, add exactly the products the picture shows. The count ("2 in this picture") is counted from the products you add.

The journal elsewhere

The Journal posts section shows the latest two to four stories on any page, from one blog and optionally one tag. Search lists stories in their own tab.
