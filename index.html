Two looks

Barathea comes with two presets. You choose one when you add the theme; each has its own demo store, filled with invented brands and products so you can see every part working.

What differs between the two looks, and how to get each one

What sets it apart

  • Story and shopA campaign picture with its products beside it. Point at a product and a fine ring shows where it is in the picture.
  • The indexMenus, lines and designers read as names; one picture follows the name under the pointer or keyboard focus.
  • Client care with contextAsked from a product, a size or the bag, the conversation already names the product, color, size and style code.
  • Pacing and the night bandPages change pace with grounds and scale, not dividers: white sections, ink sections and one night banner with its products.
  • The cardOne product card everywhere, with the line above the name, a material line, real badges and quick add by size.
  • Size before buyNothing is bought in a size nobody chose, express checkout included. Sold-out sizes lead to a person.
  • Arrange a visitA visit request through your contact form or your own booking page.
  • Promises per marketDelivery times, returns windows and duties by country, the same in the bag, on the product page and on your policy page.
  • Final saleOne quiet line, from a tag or a collection, that reaches checkout and the order.

What the theme leaves to apps

Shopify themes do not do the work of apps. Barathea has no wish list, booking calendar, back-in-stock alert or store stock lookup. It leaves clean places for apps that do; see Apps that pair well.
