Collections and lines

A line is a named group of products: a house line, a capsule, or a designer. Lines give products their top line, fill the menu panels and get pages of their own.

Lines and the Lines menu

  1. Make one collection per line (manual, or smart by tag or by vendor for designers). Give each a portrait picture and a description of 40 to 60 words; the first sentence also shows in short lists.
  2. In Online Store > Navigation, make a menu named "Lines" (or "Designers") that links to those collections. Links nested under a top link count too.
  3. Pick it in Theme settings > Lines > Lines menu.

A product's line is the first collection in that menu that holds it, top links first, in menu order. Choose what shows for products in no line with When a product is in no line: the vendor, the product type or nothing.

The line then shows:

  • above the name on cards (Product cards > Top line: Line) and on product pages (the Top line block), linked to the line;
  • in every menu panel and the phone menu (the header's Show lines in menu panels, with your own heading such as Lines or Designers);
  • at the end of each line page, as More designers in the preset (Collection links set to Lines menu, which leaves out the line being shown).

The line template

Give each line collection the template line (in the collection's admin page, under Theme template). One template serves every line: everything that differs per line comes from the collection itself.

Line intro
The line's name, intro (its description, or text you type) and picture, with an index of what the line holds: its product types with counts, or a menu. Pointing at an item swaps the picture; each item opens the grid filtered to that type, which needs the Product type filter in Search and Discovery. In a dark scheme and first on the page, the header can sit over it.
Product grid
The line's products, in four columns without tiles in the preset. Add a tile or a picture row where you want one; both print nothing until you fill them.
More designers
The rest of your Lines menu (a Collection links section; in a house with lines, title it Other lines).
Service note
Not in the preset's line template; add the section for one sentence in the service voice that opens Client care with the line as context. Write one that is true for your shop.

Per-line templates

A shared template carries one set of pictures, so a tile's picture and heading, or a Shop the look with rings, would show on every line. When you want a story picture on a line page, give that line a template of its own:

  1. In the theme editor, open the template menu at the top, choose Create template, base it on line and name it after the line (for example line-facing).
  2. Fill its Shop the look tile (picture, the products in it, top line, heading, link) and its picture row.
  3. In the collection's admin page, set its Theme template to the new template.

On the shared template, a Shop the look section set to Products: This collection uses each line's own picture and products, prints no rings and counts "From this line". With a template of its own, the section can carry its own picture and rings.

Category pages that tell a story (for example Women, Men or Bags) work the same way: make a template based on collection and fill its tiles there.

Category pages

Every other collection uses the default collection template: the Collection banner (compact, or with a picture) and the product grid, in four columns and with no tiles in the preset. Add an image tile or a picture row where a category page tells a story.

Tiles in the grid

The Product grid takes up to six tile blocks: Image tile, Text tile, Shop the look tile and Video tile.

  • Place after product counts products on each load; a full-row tile starts on the next free row.
  • Shape: tall (one column, two rows), a full row, or one card. On phones: the same shape, one card, or hidden.
  • By default a tile shows on the first load only, and hides while filters are applied, so it never lands among filtered results.

Filters and sorting

Filters come from Shopify's free Search and Discovery app; without it, availability and price are available. A good start for fashion:

  • Availability, Price, Size and Color (shown as swatches when your colors have swatch data);
  • Product type, which the line intro's index links through;
  • Vendor, labeled "Designer", for a multi-designer shop.

In the grid's settings: apply filters with an Apply button or on every change, how many values show before More, value counts, sorting, a filter bar that stays in view on phones, and up to five quick filters as buttons. Loading: a Load more button, loading while scrolling, or numbered pages.

When a collection or a filtered view is empty, the grid suggests the collections you pick in Collections to suggest.

The Collections page

The page at /collections uses the Collection list page section:

  • Grid of images: collection pictures in 2 to 4 columns.
  • Index A-Z: names grouped by letter, with letter jumps and, on wide screens, one preview picture that follows the name under the pointer or focus. The preset uses it for its Designers page.

Set Collections to From a menu and pick your Lines or Designers menu to list only those.
