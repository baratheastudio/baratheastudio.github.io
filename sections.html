Sections and blocks

Every section and block in the theme, what it is for, where it can go and each of its settings, with the names the theme editor uses.

How to read this page

Unless a section says otherwise, it also has Color scheme (which of the theme's schemes it uses) and Top spacing and Bottom spacing (None, Small, Medium or Large, scaled by Theme settings > Layout > Space between sections). Those three are left out of the tables below. A default left blank means the setting starts empty; "A sample text" means it starts with sample wording for you to replace.

  • Pictures, stories and products: Shop the look, Hero, Image banner, Two images, Three images, Large text, Featured products, Product spotlight, Collection index, Collection list, Collection links, Feature with text, Video, Journal posts

Header and footer

These sections live in the header and footer groups, so they show on every page. Change them once and every page follows.

Announcement bar notice-bar

Goes in the header group. Add it from Announcement bar.

Up to three short messages above the header, one at a time with previous and next buttons. They never change on their own. Each message can show in some countries only, so a delivery line appears only where it is true.

Header header

Part of the header group, on every page.

Logo, menu, Search, account and Bag, and the Client care link. Each top-level menu item opens a panel: its links as a text index, the lines of your Lines menu, and one portrait picture that follows the link under the pointer or keyboard focus. Menu panel blocks give each top-level item its first picture.

It has a color scheme setting and no spacing settings.

Client care panel client-care

Goes in the header group. Add it from Client care panel.

The Client care panel. It opens from the header, the phone menu, the product page, a sold-out size, quick add, the bag, the footer and search, and starts with a line about what the shopper is looking at. Blocks set the order of the ways to reach you; numbers, links and pages come from Theme settings > Client care, and each item hides while its setting is empty. See Client care with context.

It has a color scheme setting and no spacing settings.

Newsletter pop-up newsletter-popup

Goes on any page and in the footer group. Add it from Newsletter pop-up.

An email signup in a small dialog, off until you turn it on. It waits the delay you set, comes back only after the days you set, and never shows to signed-in customers who already accept email. Add it to the footer group to offer it on every page.

It has a color scheme setting and no spacing settings.

The sections that carry campaign pictures and the products in them. Most home pages are built from these.

Shop the look story-shop

Goes on any page. Add it from Shop the look.

A campaign picture and the products in it, as one section. Pointing at a product, or reaching it with the keyboard, draws a fine ring where it sits in the picture, and the credit counts the products ("3 in this picture"). Placed first on the home page, its heading is the page heading. See Story and shop.
