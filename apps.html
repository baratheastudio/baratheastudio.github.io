Good to know

Apps that pair well

Shopify asks themes to leave app work to apps. Barathea does, and leaves a clean place where each kind of app fits.

Where apps go

  • App blocks go in Product information, Featured product, the Story section of journal articles and the Bag page, beside the theme's own blocks.
  • The Apps section holds app blocks on any page, the header and footer groups included.
  • App embeds (chat buttons, pop-ups, tracking) are turned on in the theme editor, in its App embeds panel.
  • Wherever an app block can go, a Custom Liquid block can too, for snippets an app asks you to paste.

Kinds of apps, and their place

Wish list
Not built in. Add the app's block to Product information next to Buy buttons, and its embed for a header link if it has one.
Back-in-stock alerts
Not built in. Add the app's block under Buy buttons. The theme's own path for a sold-out size is Ask about this size, which opens Client care with the product and size.
Booking
Themes may not take bookings. Paste the address of your booking app's page into Booking link in Theme settings > Client care, and every Arrange a visit link goes there. Without one, Arrange a visit opens your contact form with the visit topic.
Reviews
Review apps fill the rating metafields the theme reads (reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count) for the Star rating block and, if you turn it on, the rating on cards. Add the app's review list as an app block or in an Apps section.
Fit and size advice, try-on
App blocks in Product information, near Colors and sizes.
Store stock lookup
The theme shows Shopify's own pickup availability for the chosen size. Lookups across stores need an app.
Filters and complementary products
Shopify's free Search and Discovery app: filters for collections and search, and the products for Complete the look.
Translation
Shopify's free Translate and Adapt app, or Online Store > Themes > ... > Edit default theme content. See Translations and right-to-left.
Email marketing
The theme's signup forms add customers with email marketing consent to Shopify. Email apps pick them up from there.

Apps and speed

Each app adds its own code to your pages. If your store slows down, turn app embeds off one at a time in the editor and compare. Remove the blocks and embeds of apps you uninstall.

Questions about an app's own block or embed belong with its developer. If something looks like a theme problem, write to us and we will say so; see Support.
